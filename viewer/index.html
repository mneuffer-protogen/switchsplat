    SwitchSplat

      Controls
      • Click a part: open or close it (animated)
      • Drag a part: swing or slide it, release to let it coast. A shut door lets go of its latch a moment after you take hold
      • Shift + move the mouse over a part: push it
      • Lock a part (button, or in edit mode): it only rattles when pulled or clicked
      • Drag empty space: orbit. Right drag: pan. Wheel: zoom
      • VR: grip next to a part to take hold (a latched door needs a grab, not a push), trigger to toggle it, push with the glowing hand spheres. Left stick walks, right stick snap-turns
      • Edit mode: move the pivot (orange), turn the axis, tune the joint, download scene.json
